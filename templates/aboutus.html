State Poverty Eradication Mission

Who We Are & What We Stand For

Kudumbashree (meaning 'Prosperity of the Family' in Malayalam) was set up in 1998 to eradicate absolute poverty through concerted community action under the leadership of Local Self Governments.

Through Neighborhood Groups (NHGs), Kudumbashree enables women to build sustainable micro-enterprises, hone traditional crafts, and bring high-quality organic food, handicrafts, and handlooms directly to your doorstep.
